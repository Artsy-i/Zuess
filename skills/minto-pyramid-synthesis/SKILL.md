# Minto Pyramid Strategic Synthesis Specification

## 1. Architecture of Thought: The Minto Pyramid
Every chapter and analytical section must be structured strictly top-down:
1. **The Governing Conclusion (Answer First):** Lead directly with the definitive strategic finding or commercial reality in the opening sentence. Do NOT build up to the answer.
2. **Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive (MECE) Supporting Pillars:** Group empirical evidence into 2–3 logically distinct pillars (e.g., Technological Constraints vs. Capex Concentration vs. Geopolitical Chokepoints).
3. **Quantitative Grounding:** Every claim in every paragraph must be anchored to a concrete metric extracted by Node A.

## 2. SCQA Structural Execution
Open each major analytical section using the SCQA rhythm:
- **Situation (S):** State the undisputed operational baseline (e.g., "Advanced packaging has shifted from post-fab assembly to the core determinant of compute density.").
- **Complication (C):** Introduce the friction, capacity ceiling, or cost barrier (e.g., "Sub-10µm hybrid bonding yields drop precipitously below 85% when die sizes exceed 800mm², creating severe supply bottlenecks.").
- **Question (Q):** Frame the implicit enterprise dilemma (e.g., "How do hyperscalers bypass silicon interposer size constraints while preserving thermal budgets?").
- **Answer (A):** The synthesized strategic conclusion backed by operational data.

## 3. Style & Tone Standards (Institutional B2B)
- **Zero Throat-Clearing:** Delete phrases like "In this chapter, we will explore...", "It is important to remember...", or "As stated previously...".
- **Dense Prose:** Combine cause, effect, and metric in single, high-impact statements:
  * Weak: "Packaging is getting more expensive. Companies are spending a lot of money to upgrade equipment."
  * Institutional: "Surging silicon interposer costs—representing up to 22% of total packaged module BOM—have forced tier-1 OSATs to commit $14.2B in cumulative 2025 Capex toward glass substrate transition lines."
- **Visual Scannability:** Interleave technical prose with clean Markdown comparison tables (Entity | Metric | Baseline | Strategic Implication).
